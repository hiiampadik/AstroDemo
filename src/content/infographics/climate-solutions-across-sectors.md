## What does the chart show?

Energy, industry, transport, buildings and agriculture each emit in their own
way — and each has its own mix of solutions, from clean electricity to changed
land use.

## Why it matters

There is no single fix. Deep decarbonisation means pursuing many sector-specific
changes at once, reinforcing one another across the whole economy.
