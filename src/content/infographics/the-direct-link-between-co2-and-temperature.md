## What does the chart show?

Across hundreds of thousands of years reconstructed from ice cores, atmospheric
CO₂ and temperature rise and fall together. The relationship is one of the most
robust in all of climate science.

## Why it matters

This tight coupling is why adding CO₂ to the atmosphere so reliably raises the
temperature — and why cutting emissions is the most direct lever we have on
future warming.
