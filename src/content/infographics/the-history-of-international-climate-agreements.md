## What does the chart show?

From the first world climate conferences to the Kyoto Protocol and the Paris
Agreement, a timeline of the institutions and treaties through which countries
have tried to coordinate action.

## Why it matters

Progress has been uneven, but the framework built over these decades is how
nearly every nation now shares goals, reports emissions and ratchets ambition.
