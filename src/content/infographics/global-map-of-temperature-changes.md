## What does the chart show?

Mapping warming across the surface reveals a patchwork: land heats faster than
sea, and the high Arctic faster than anywhere else — a pattern known as polar
amplification.

## Why it matters

Because impacts follow local temperature, not the global average, some regions
are already experiencing changes far beyond the headline figure of 1.3 °C.
