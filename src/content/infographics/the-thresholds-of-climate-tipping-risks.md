## What does the chart show?

For each major tipping element, scientists estimate the temperature range within
which it may cross its threshold. Several of those ranges begin close to today's
level of warming.

## Why it matters

Staying well below 2 °C — and closer to 1.5 °C — keeps most of these systems on
the safe side of their thresholds. Every tenth of a degree lowers the risk.
