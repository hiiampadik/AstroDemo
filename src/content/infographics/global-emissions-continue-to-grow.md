## What does the chart show?

Despite decades of climate summits, global emissions have kept climbing, driven
by growing energy demand still met largely by fossil fuels.

## Why it matters

Warming responds to cumulative emissions, so as long as the annual total stays
high, the atmosphere's carbon burden — and the temperature — keeps rising.
