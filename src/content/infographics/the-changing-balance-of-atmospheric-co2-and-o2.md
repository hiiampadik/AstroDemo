## What does the chart show?

As plants grow through spring and summer they draw down CO₂ and release oxygen;
in autumn and winter the process reverses. This seasonal breathing of the
biosphere is visible as a yearly zig-zag in both gases.

## Why it matters

Beneath the annual cycle runs a steady trend: CO₂ climbs and O₂ falls in near
lock-step, the unmistakable fingerprint of burning fossil carbon rather than any
natural source.
