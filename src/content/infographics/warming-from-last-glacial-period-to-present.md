## What does the chart show?

Placing today's warming alongside the slow thaw out of the last ice age puts the
present moment in perspective: a change that once unfolded over millennia is now
happening within a single century.

## Why it matters

It is not only how much the climate is changing that matters, but how fast. The
speed of modern warming leaves little time for ecosystems, agriculture and
infrastructure to adjust.
