## A long-settled science

The greenhouse effect of carbon dioxide was described in the nineteenth
century, and by the early twentieth scientists had already estimated how much
warming a doubling of atmospheric CO₂ would bring. What was once a theoretical
calculation is now measured directly, year after year.

## Why it matters

The physics behind climate change is not new or contested. More than a century
of research — from laboratory measurements to global observation networks —
points to the same conclusion, giving today's projections a deep and durable
foundation.
