## What does the chart show?

The European Union has cut emissions substantially while its economy grew,
showing that the two can be decoupled — though transport has lagged behind the
other sectors.

## Why it matters

The EU's trajectory is a proof of concept that emissions can fall through policy
and investment, and a reminder that the hardest sectors need extra attention.
