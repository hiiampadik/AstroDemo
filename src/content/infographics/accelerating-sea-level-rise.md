## What does the chart show?

Seas rise for two main reasons: warmer water expands, and melting glaciers and
ice sheets add fresh water to the ocean. Both are accelerating, so the curve
steepens over time.

## Why it matters

Even modest rise multiplies the reach of storm surges and coastal flooding, and
because the ocean responds slowly, today's emissions commit us to centuries of
further rise.
