## What does the chart show?

Different policy pathways lead to very different futures — from around 1.5 °C if
pledges are fully met and strengthened, to well over 3 °C if little changes.

## Why it matters

The gap between these paths is the space where policy choices act. The outcome in
2100 is not fixed; it depends on decisions taken this decade.
