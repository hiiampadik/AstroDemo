## What does the chart show?

There is a finite amount of CO₂ humanity can still emit while keeping a good
chance of holding warming to 1.5 °C. At current rates that budget shrinks by the
year and is nearly gone.

## Why it matters

The budget framing makes the stakes concrete: limiting warming is not about a
distant end date but about how quickly emissions fall, starting now.
