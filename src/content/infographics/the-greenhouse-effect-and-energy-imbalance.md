## What does the chart show?

Sunlight warms the Earth, which radiates heat back toward space. Greenhouse
gases intercept some of that outgoing heat, keeping the planet habitable — but
more gas means more trapped energy.

## Why it matters

Today the Earth absorbs slightly more energy than it releases. That persistent
imbalance is the engine of warming, and it will continue until greenhouse gas
concentrations stop rising.
