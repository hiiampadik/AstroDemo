## What does the chart show?

Carbon dioxide accounts for the largest share of warming, but methane, nitrous
oxide and fluorinated gases add substantially — some trapping far more heat per
tonne, though they linger for different lengths of time.

## Why it matters

Different gases call for different solutions. Cutting short-lived methane can
slow warming quickly, while reining in long-lived CO₂ is essential for the
long run.
