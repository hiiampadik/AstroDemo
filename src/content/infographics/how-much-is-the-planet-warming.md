## What does the chart show?

Global average surface temperature is compared against the pre-industrial
baseline of 1850–1900. Individual years wobble with natural variability, but the
long-term line climbs unmistakably upward.

## Why it matters

A quarter of a degree per decade sounds small, yet it is roughly ten times faster
than the natural warming that ended the last ice age — fast enough to outpace the
ability of many ecosystems to adapt.
