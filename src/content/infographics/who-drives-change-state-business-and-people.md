## What does the chart show?

States set the rules and prices, businesses build and deploy the technologies,
and people shift demand through the choices they make and the changes they
demand.

## Why it matters

None of the three can decarbonise alone. Durable progress comes when policy,
markets and public support pull in the same direction.
