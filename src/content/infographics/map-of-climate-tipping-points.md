## What does the chart show?

Several large parts of the climate system — ice sheets, ocean currents, forests —
can flip into a new state once pushed past a threshold, sometimes irreversibly.

## Why it matters

Tipping points turn gradual warming into abrupt, self-reinforcing change. Their
exact thresholds are uncertain, which is precisely why they are so dangerous.
