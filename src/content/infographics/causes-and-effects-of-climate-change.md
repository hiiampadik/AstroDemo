## What does the chart show?

Human activity — above all the burning of fossil fuels — releases greenhouse
gases into the atmosphere. A higher concentration of these gases traps more
heat, setting off a long chain of interconnected impacts across the whole
climate system: warming oceans, melting ice, rising seas and more frequent
extreme weather.

## Why it matters

Each link in the chain reinforces the next. Understanding climate change as a
system of causes and effects — rather than a single number on a thermometer —
is the first step to grasping both its scale and the leverage points where it
can be slowed.
